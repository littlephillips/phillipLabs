Qwetu Movers is a moving and storage company operating around Nairobi. Before we worked together, their only online presence was a Facebook page, every quote request came in as a comment or a DM, and a lot of them got missed.

## The problem

Three things were costing them jobs:

- No way for a customer to describe a move (size, distance, date) without typing it all out in a DM
- Quote requests scattered across comments, DMs, and phone calls with no single place to track them
- No trust signals — a new customer landing on the Facebook page had no way to quickly see what past jobs looked like

## What we built

A focused, single-purpose site: a homepage that explains the service in one scroll, a simple quote form that captures the details up front, and a WhatsApp handoff so the conversation continues somewhere qwetumovers team already lives.

We deliberately kept the first version small, homepage, services, and the quote flow rather than trying to build a full logistics platform on day one.

## The result

Quote requests now arrive as structured messages instead of scattered comments, which makes them much faster to respond to. The team can point ad traffic straight at the quote form instead of "message us on Facebook."
