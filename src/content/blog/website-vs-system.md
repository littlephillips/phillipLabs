A lot of business owners come to me asking for "a website" when what's actually costing them time and money is the lack of a **system** — something that funnels in clinet, tracks orders, bookings, and gives them inline presence. Knowing which one you need first saves money.

## A website's job

A website's only job is to answer three questions fast: what do you do, why should I trust you, and how do I contact you. That's it. If you don't have one yet, this is almost always where to start — it's the cheapest, fastest thing to ship.

## A system's job

A system runs a process for you — tracking who booked what, who paid, what's in stock. You need this once a spreadsheet or a WhatsApp thread stops being enough to keep the business organised.

## How to tell which one you need

Ask yourself: is the pain "customers can't find me" or "I can't keep track of what's happening in my own business"? The first is a website problem. The second is a system problem. Most businesses hit the first pain before the second — which is exactly why the [Bronze and Silver packages](/#pricing) exist as a starting point, with room to add a CRM later once you actually need one.
